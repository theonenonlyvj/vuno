# Classic Uno Principles Used By VUno

Sources: the user-provided rules paste and `http://unorules.com/`.

## First-Order Rules

1. The deck is the original 108-card deck: four colors, number cards, Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, four Wild cards, and four Wild Draw Four cards.
2. The objective of a round is to empty your hand. The objective of the game is to reach 500 points first.
3. A legal play matches the current color, the top card's number, the top card's action symbol, or is a Wild card.
4. A turn has one card play at most. Stacking and multi-card discards are outside classic play.
5. Drawing is one-card-at-a-time turn resolution: if the drawn card is playable, the player may play it; otherwise the player keeps it and the turn passes.
6. Action cards affect the next player in turn order unless Reverse changes the direction first.
7. Wild chooses the next color. Wild Draw Four chooses the next color and penalizes the next player, but is legal only when the player has no card matching the current color.
8. UNO is a state transition, not decoration: reaching one card without calling it costs two cards.
9. Scoring happens after a player empties their hand, including any forced draws caused by the final card.

## Deliberate Exclusions

VUno excludes later or house-rule mechanics: stacking Draw cards, jump-in play, Seven-Zero, forced swaps, shuffle hands, blank custom cards, teams, remote multiplayer, and alternate elimination scoring.
